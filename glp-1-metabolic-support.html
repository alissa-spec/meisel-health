The Science

What is GLP-1?

GLP-1 is a natural hormone your body already makes. It helps regulate appetite, signal fullness, support blood sugar balance, and slow digestion so you stay satisfied longer.

GLP-1 medications are designed to support and enhance this natural process.

In simple terms, that means your body may stop feeling like it is working against you all day.
